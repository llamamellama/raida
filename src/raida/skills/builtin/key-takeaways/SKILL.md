Organize the important content of the sources, then show how to put it into practice.

1. Core ideas: the main teachings or insights, each in one or two sentences, with the anchor of the passage it comes from. Quote the speaker or author where a short quote says it best.
2. Steps and practices: concrete actions a reader can take, in a sensible order. For each, say what to do, when or how often, and which idea from the sources it applies. Suggest only practices the sources support.
3. Reminders: a few short points to hold on to when it gets difficult, drawn from the sources.

Write for someone who wants to use this, not to study it: plain words and short paragraphs.
