Write an article for a general reader, based only on the sources.

- A title that makes the subject clear, and a lead paragraph that says what the article is about and why it matters.
- Three to five sections with short headings, in an order that builds the argument or story.
- A short conclusion that draws the threads together without adding new claims.
- Cite the anchor of a passage in brackets where a fact or quote needs support; do not cite every sentence.
- About 800 words unless the additional instructions say otherwise.
