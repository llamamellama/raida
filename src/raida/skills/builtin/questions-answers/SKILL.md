Write the questions a thoughtful reader would ask about the sources, and answer each one from them.

- Eight to twelve questions unless the additional instructions say otherwise, from basic to deeper, covering the whole of the sources.
- Answer each in two to five sentences, with the anchors of the passages the answer comes from.
- When the sources do not answer a question fully, say what they leave open instead of filling the gap.
