Write the minutes of the meeting in the sources.

- Start with the meeting's subject, date and participants when the sources give them; do not guess what they do not say.
- List every decision, each with the time code or anchor where it was made.
- List the action items in a table: action, owner, due date and anchor. Leave a cell empty rather than inventing an owner or a date.
- Summarize the discussion by topic, briefly, keeping the positions people took and the reasons they gave.
- End with open issues and the next steps agreed.
