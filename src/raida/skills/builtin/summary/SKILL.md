Summarize the sources in this session.

- Open with a short overview of two to four sentences: what the sources are, who speaks or wrote them, and what they are about.
- Then give the key points, grouped by theme rather than by source. Put the anchor of the passage each point comes from after it. When there are several sources, say which one each point comes from.
- Include up to five notable quotes, word for word, with their anchors.
- End with the questions the sources leave open, if there are any.
- Keep it to about 600 words unless the additional instructions say otherwise.
